About the Podcast

God’s Love Above All is a faith-centered podcast focused on putting God first and growing in a deeper relationship with Him. Each episode shares reflections from Scripture, encouragement for everyday life, and reminders of God’s love and truth. This space is meant to help you stay rooted in Christ and strengthen your faith.

The Mission

The mission of God’s Love Above All is to encourage hearts to seek God, stay rooted in Christ, and grow in faith through His Word.

About the Host

Ivette is the heart behind God’s Love Above All, sharing Christ-centered encouragement, biblical reflection, and reminders to keep God first in everyday life. Through this podcast, she hopes to help others stay rooted in faith, grow closer to Christ, and find peace in God’s truth.
